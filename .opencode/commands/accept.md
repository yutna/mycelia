# Accept

For pull request $1, collect the diff summary, the linked issue, the check
results, and the evidence. Produce:

1. What changed and why.
2. Risk tier and affected areas.
3. Evidence and check results.
4. Rollback plan.
5. Open questions.
6. A recommendation: accept, revise, or block.

Do not merge.
